# v0.4.11.0 — Power Ability & Bronze Breakthrough Progression

## Power Ability Association

Every Power defaults to the associated ability of the Essence through which the character owns it.

A Power may define one optional alternate ability. If it does, the player permanently chooses:
- the source Essence ability, or
- the Power's alternate ability.

Example:
- Cleave owned through Blade defaults to Dexterity because Blade is Dexterity-based.
- Cleave may define Strength as its alternate.
- The player chooses Dexterity or Strength for that owned Cleave.
- Cleave owned through Might defaults to Strength; if Strength is also the alternate, no choice is needed.

The resolved Power ability drives Power Attack and Essence/chosen ability modifier references.

## Iron -> Bronze

Iron Power progression ends at Iron 9.

Once all five Powers in an Essence are Iron 9:
- Bronze breakthrough unlocks for those five Powers.
- Each Iron 9 -> Bronze 0 purchase costs 150 XP.
- Powers may cross one at a time.
- The Essence remains Iron 9 until all five Powers are Bronze 0.
- Once all five are Bronze 0, the Essence becomes Bronze 0.

Bronze 1+ is locked until the whole Essence is Bronze 0.

## Bronze Power XP

- Bronze 0 breakthrough: 150 XP
- Bronze 1: 25 XP
- Bronze 2: 30 XP
- Bronze 3: 40 XP
- Bronze 4: 50 XP
- Bronze 5: 65 XP
- Bronze 6: 80 XP
- Bronze 7: 100 XP
- Bronze 8: 125 XP
- Bronze 9: 150 XP

Character Rank continues to use four Essences and cannot cross a tier until all four Essences have crossed it.

No separate Essence or Character XP purchase exists; those ranks are derived from Power progression.
